# Materials Acceptance Record — Item 054: Block A Slab-on-Grade Fill Compaction Testing (Field Density Report SF-26-0708)

Completed technical materials acceptance record `/filesystem/Thelbridge_Materials_Acceptance_Fill_Compaction_2026-0708.docx` following the Thelbridge Materials Acceptance Record template, dated 07/20/2026, from Karen Tarbray (Materials / QA Reviewer) to Adrian Vance (Project Engineer), copied to Rachel Marlbeck (Project Director), retaining the five standard sections (`1. PURPOSE`, `2. DOCUMENTS REVIEWED`, `3. BASIS OF REVIEW`, `4. FINDINGS`, `5. CONCLUSION AND RECOMMENDATION`):

1. **Materials Acceptance Disposition & Location Evaluation**:
   - Test locations FD-14 through FD-17 are **REJECTED** on the project record as it stands.
   - Field Density Report SF-26-0708 stated its specification requirement as a density criterion of 95 percent of the reference maximum dry density and dispositioned all four test locations as PASS, but did so against invalid laboratory reference standards.
   - Acceptance under Specification Section 31 20 00 Article 3.4.B requires both the density criterion (≥ 95% Modified Proctor MDD) and the moisture criterion (optimum moisture ± 2.0 percentage points) to be satisfied at each test location.

2. **Missing ASTM D1557 Method C Determination & Undetermined Limits**:
   - The ASTM D1557 Method C determination required for test locations FD-14 through FD-17 has yet to be performed on the material placed there.
   - Consequently, the density floor and moisture window of Specification Section 31 20 00 Article 3.4.B remain undetermined for test locations FD-14 through FD-17 on the project record as it stands.
   - An ASTM D1557 Method C determination on the material actually placed is required before test locations FD-14 through FD-17 can be evaluated.

3. **Material Source, RFI 079 & Register Item 012 Scope**:
   - The structural fill placed in the Block A slab-on-grade pad was drawn from the stockpile north of Block C.
   - RFI 079 approved the stockpile north of Block C for reuse subject to a gradation retest (via letter TPM-25-079).
   - The gradation retest required by RFI 079 remains outstanding in the project record.
   - Thelbridge Verification Register Item 012 was closed on 09/22/2025, but the scope of Thelbridge Verification Register Item 012 is limited to the Larchwood Pit borrow.
   - Vantree Report VMT-25-0918-P04 was performed on Larchwood Pit material, a different source from the stockpile north of Block C.

4. **Specification Standard Errors & Applied Denominators**:
   - Specification Section 31 20 00 Article 1.2.A.1 cites ASTM D698 for historical borrow characterization only.
   - Specification Section 31 20 00 Article 1.2.A.2 names ASTM D1557 as the governing laboratory reference for Package A structural fill.
   - Specification Section 31 20 00 Article 2.1.D requires an ASTM D1557 Method C determination for each borrow source and for each material change prior to placement.
   - Specification Section 31 20 00 Articles 1.4.C and 3.4.D require the acceptance denominators to be those determined on the material actually placed at each test location.
   - Field Density Report SF-26-0708 computed percent compaction against a maximum dry density of 118.6 pcf determined under ASTM D698 on the Phase 1 Wexford Ridge borrow.

5. **Lift Non-Conformance & Reissuance Requirement**:
   - The lift covered by Field Density Report SF-26-0708 is non-conforming under Specification Section 31 20 00 Article 3.7.C.
   - Field Density Report SF-26-0708 must be reissued against a new ASTM D1557 Method C determination on the material actually placed.

6. **Subgrade Preparation & Field Testing Frequency**:
   - The subgrade beneath the Block A slab-on-grade pad was proof-rolled and accepted in writing before the first lift was placed (Inspection Record SR-26-0612).
   - The field density testing frequency required by Specification Section 31 20 00 was satisfied on each lift of the Block A slab-on-grade pad (4 tests on 18,000 sq ft satisfies 1 test per 5,000 sq ft).

7. **Continuous Special Inspection Deficiency**:
   - The adopted building code requires continuous special inspection during the placement and compaction of the Block A slab-on-grade structural fill, and the Laboratory's attendance on 07/08/2026, 07:15 to 11:30, for density testing does not satisfy it (IBC §1705.6 requires continuous oversight during spreading, moisture conditioning, and rolling).

8. **Construction Gate & Verification Register Status**:
   - Construction over the Block A slab-on-grade fill may proceed only after a new ASTM D1557 Method C determination on the material actually placed is obtained and the fill is accepted under Specification Section 31 20 00 Article 3.4.B.
   - Thelbridge Verification Register Item 054 remains open pending the actions the record requires.
   - Verification Register Item 054 remains outstanding at its 07/20/2026 due date.
