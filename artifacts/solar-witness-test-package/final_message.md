# Municipal Witness Test Package — Determination and Package Filing

## Executive Summary
The DER Program Office has completed the review of the municipal witness-test waiver batch as of July 14, 2025, covering all six originally queued arrays and processing the five active municipal solar installations.

### Scope and In-Scope Array Count
- **Batch Scope**: Five (5) municipal arrays are in active scope for this batch on July 14, 2025:
  1. Cravenwood High School
  2. Darnwick Elementary School
  3. Holcott Library
  4. Lockmere Middle School
  5. Marden Falls Water Reclamation Facility (WRF)
- **Withdrawal**: Tresbay Community Center formally withdrew from the municipal batch on July 09, 2025, as recorded in case tracking (Case 45).

### Certified Equipment List (CEL) Carriage Determinations
1. **Cravenwood High School**: Solmery Power SM-215K-3P inverter is carried on the current Certified Equipment List (CEL-2025-Q2).
2. **Marden Falls WRF**: Solmery Power SM-245K-3P inverter is carried on the current Certified Equipment List (CEL-2025-Q2).
3. **Darnwick Elementary School**: Arvexa PV-750 inverter is NOT carried on the Certified Equipment List (CEL-2025-Q2).
4. **Holcott Library**: Selvix SV-720 inverter is NOT carried on the Certified Equipment List (CEL-2025-Q2).
5. **Lockmere Middle School**: Power conversion equipment falls outside the current Certified Equipment List. Although the original application listed a carried model, field verification confirmed the installation of the Selvix SV-720 inverter, which is NOT carried on the Certified Equipment List.

### System Operations Center (SOC) Release List
- **Release List**: The list of arrays that the System Operations Center (SOC) can be asked to release is **EMPTY** (0 arrays).
- **Governing Basis**: Under DIS-01 Rev 4 Section 6.4, waiver of witness testing requires an affirmative, signed disposition by Standards Engineering. No array in this batch carries a signed Standards Engineering waiver disposition. Discretionary waiver authority rests solely with Standards Engineering.
- **Additional Release Conditions**: Cravenwood High School has an additional unfulfilled condition: live telemetry remains open, as no RTU has been delivered and commissioning checklist Section 5 remains open.

### Witness Testing Plan for Unlisted Arrays
For the three arrays whose inverters fall outside the Certified Equipment List (Darnwick Elementary, Holcott Library, and Lockmere Middle School):
- **Required Tests**: Mandatory witnessed commissioning tests per ENG-PRC-014 §4.2, DIS-01 Rev 4 §6, and IEEE 1547.1, including protective trip tests, anti-islanding protection, and voltage/frequency ride-through verification.
- **Performance**: The customer or customer's qualified testing contractor/agent performs all test procedures.
- **Witness**: An Alleniom Utilities witness engineer witnesses and verifies test execution.
- **Pre-Test Records**: The following records must be on file prior to scheduling witness testing:
  1. Completed and signed commissioning checklist;
  2. Manufacturer factory test report / certificate of conformance;
  3. Approved site-specific witness test procedure.
