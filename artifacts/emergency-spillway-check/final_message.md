# Technical Review Memorandum — Phase 3 Detention Basin Emergency Spillway (Comment G-4)

Completed technical review memorandum `/filesystem/Memo_to_Callaway_Spillway_2026-10-31.docx` on Sundial Engineering & Survey letterhead, dated 10/31/2026, addressed to Garrett Nokes (Callaway Land Partners), signed by Max Braddock, Project Manager. The memorandum resolves Dale's plan check comment G-4 regarding freeboard, sizing, apron, and outfall routing for the Phase 3 detention basin emergency spillway on Sheet C-9:

1. **Right of Discharge & Windmill Lane Acceptance**:
   - No document in the project record grants a right to discharge emergency spillway overflow into the Windmill Lane curb and gutter that Clark County Public Works has accepted.
   - The Windmill Lane half-street improvements receiving overflow were accepted by Clark County Public Works as part of Phase 2 offsite improvements (punch list closed).

2. **Sheet C-9 Deficiencies & Required Additions**:
   - Sheet C-9 as drawn is incomplete for responding to comment G-4; it lacks emergency spillway sizing calculations, a dedicated spillway cross-section, and explicit freeboard dimension strings.
   - Sheet C-9 must be revised to include formal spillway sizing calculations and a detailed spillway section to satisfy comment G-4.

3. **Crest Elevation & Height Discrepancies**:
   - Detail callout gives 0.71 ft and basin notes give 0.60 ft for the height of the spillway crest above the 100-year water surface elevation (actual elevation difference between crest El. 2,858.50 and 100-year WSEL El. 2,857.91 is 0.59 ft).

4. **Master Drainage Study Hydrology & Outflow**:
   - Revised basin notes cite a Master Drainage Study (MDS) outflow of 41.5 cfs at the emergency spillway crest elevation (Table D-2: Phase 3 basin at El. 2,858.50 with 7.12 ac-ft storage and 41.5 cfs outflow, compared to 27.9 cfs at the 100-year WSEL).
   - Flow passing over the emergency spillway bypasses the metered outlet control structure (12"x18" rectangular orifice plate) that limits Phase 3 discharge to the 78 cfs cap at the shared downstream outfall.

5. **Apron Specification Gaps**:
   - Sheet C-9 specifies the spillway apron as 15 ft by 20 ft of grouted rip-rap with D50 of 12 inches, but omits rip-rap thickness, bedding material, geotextile filter fabric, and cutoff wall depth dimensions.

6. **Downstream Flow Path & Offsite Routing**:
   - Overflow passing over the emergency spillway discharges across Common-Area Parcel E before reaching the Windmill Lane gutter line.

7. **Freeboard Invalidation**:
   - The 1.29 ft freeboard reported in the basin notes does not hold once the emergency spillway operates (crest is at El. 2,858.50 ft; once the spillway is engaged, remaining clearance to the top of berm at El. 2,859.20 ft is less than 0.70 ft, decreasing to zero at design head).

8. **Master Drainage Study Regulatory Status**:
   - The proposed spillway revisions do NOT trigger a formal amendment to the accepted Master Drainage Study, as tributary drainage area, basin location, storage volume, and the 78 cfs peak outfall cap remain unchanged.
