# Verification Memorandum — Item 048: Block B Plant-Room Excavation Shoring Design (Submittal 31-SH-B Rev.2)

Completed technical verification memorandum `/filesystem/Thelbridge_Verification_Memo_Item048_BlockB.docx` following the Thelbridge memorandum form, dated 07/20/2026, addressed to Rachel Marlbeck (Project Director) from A. Vance (Project Engineer), retaining the five standard sections (`1. PURPOSE`, `2. DOCUMENTS REVIEWED`, `3. BASIS OF REVIEW`, `4. FINDINGS`, `5. CONCLUSION AND RECOMMENDATION`):

1. **Retained Height & Lateral Load Case Invalidation**:
   - The retained height at the plant-room location is about 8.5 ft.
   - The working behind the retained height at the plant room is the basement floor at the top of the mat, El. 734.5, less the plant-room subgrade, El. 726.0 (734.5 ft − 726.0 ft = 8.5 ft).
   - The lateral load case of Supplement Section R2.3 cannot be carried forward as the design loading for this excavation.
   - No free water surface at El. 742.0 can act behind the sheet-pile wall at the plant-room location (the pit is inside the basement where surrounding floor slab is at El. 734.5 ft).
   - The 250 psf construction surcharge is not the loading that acts behind the wall at the plant-room location (civil traffic cannot run inside basement).
   - The toe-embedment, anchor-load, sheet-flexure and deflection checks of Supplement Sections R2.4 to R2.7 cannot be carried forward because each takes its demand from the R2.3 lateral load case, which is built on a working grade and a hydrostatic column that do not exist at the plant-room location.

2. **Constructability Clashes & Permanent Work Impacts**:
   - The plant-room excavation outline lies inside the extent of the Blocks A/B basement mat.
   - No opening in the continuous basement mat over the plant-room pit is shown in the project record.
   - The tieback anchor row at El. 745.5 lies inside the completed basement (11.0 ft in the air above the El. 734.5 basement slab).
   - A 30.5 ft sheet pile cannot be driven at the plant-room location with the plant the design relies on (under 15 ft basement ceiling).
   - No driveability analysis supports the revised sheet-pile tip elevation of El. 719.5 (refusal stratum).
   - The excavation undermines the Block B pile caps at underside El. 732.0 (subgrade El. 726.0 ft is 6.0 ft below cap underside).
   - No temporary support of the basement mat and the pile caps during the excavation is designed in Rev.1 or Rev.2.

3. **Geotechnical Capacity Shortfalls**:
   - The undrained shear strength of 1,400 psf used in the toe check is not supported by the project record (site logs report su = 950 to 1,050 psf).
   - The sheet-pile toe embedment does not reach the 1.50 factor of safety when checked on the undrained strength the geotechnical report reports (FS = 1.18 < 1.50).
   - The tieback bond zone lies within the CL clay aquitard rather than the SW-GW stratum whose bond stress is adopted.

4. **Basal Heave, Dewatering & Aquitard Consolidation**:
   - No sealed dewatering design covering the Block B relief wells exists in the project record.
   - Without relief-well pumping the base-heave factor of safety is about 0.58 (downward clay weight W = 720 psf vs upward artesian uplift u = 1,248 psf).
   - The piezometer stop-work trigger at El. 727.0 does not satisfy the base-heave minimum the design basis memorandum requires (DBM §6.2 mandates FS ≥ 1.50, which is violated above El. 726.9 ft).
   - Holding the confined piezometric head at El. 726.5 raises the effective stress in the clay aquitard beneath the completed Block B work, whether stated in those terms or shown by carrying the pore-pressure reduction onto the in-situ vertical effective stress (pore pressure drop Δu = 967 psf transfers an effective stress increase Δσ' = +967 psf).
   - The effect of the excavation and of the aquifer depressurization on the Block B piles, pile caps and basement mat has not been evaluated by any party.

5. **Contractual Authority & Required Submittals**:
   - Specification Section 31 50 00, cited as the authority for the delegated shoring design, is not in the project record.
   - Prerequisite deliverables and responsible parties before excavation can start:
     1. Revised delegated shoring submittal 31-SH-B by Kesterline Engineering, PC transmitted via Rowan Bay Constructors, Inc.
     2. Sealed Block B dewatering and groundwater-control plan under Specification 31 23 19 by Rowan Bay Constructors, Inc. with its specialist dewatering designer.
     3. Aquifer depressurization settlement impact evaluation on completed Block B foundation works by SER (Calloway Braddock) and GER (Kelbrook).
