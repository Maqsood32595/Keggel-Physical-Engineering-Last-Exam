# Pre-Application Capacity Response — Feeder ALN-108 (Elderpine Substation)

## Executive Summary & Official Issuance
The **DER Program Office of Alleniom Utilities** has finalized and issued the official written pre-application response to Northline Renewables LLC on **July 14, 2025** (well ahead of the August 15, 2025 League response deadline). This response addresses Northline Renewables' inquiry dated July 2, 2025 regarding a proposed 3 MW pure-generation photovoltaic facility (Elderpine Solar I) on Feeder ALN-108.

### Core Determinations and Tariff-Defensible Grounds
1. **Available Capacity Confirmation Withheld**:
   - **Determination**: Formal confirmation of currently available capacity for Northline Renewables on Feeder ALN-108 as of July 14, 2025 is **WITHHELD** pending confirmation of the current feeder state by Distribution Planning.
   - **Grounds**: Under Regulatory Affairs Directive **RA-2025-07-01 Section 3.7**, no published hosting-capacity map figure may be communicated externally without first confirming that the figure reflects the current state of the feeder. The published map figure (3.0 MW on HCM-2024-Q2, dated 05/15/2024) does not incorporate subsequent queue additions and cannot be outwardly confirmed.
2. **Mandatory Condition on Project-Sizing Reliance**:
   - **Determination**: All project-sizing reliance by Northline Renewables on an available capacity figure for Feeder ALN-108 is **STRICTLY CONDITIONAL** on Distribution Planning first confirming the current interim position through a formal engineering assessment.
   - **Grounds**: Post-Publication Planning Study **PLN-ALN-108-2024-10 Section 1 and Section 5** (Recommendations 1 and 4).
3. **Purely Illustrative Feeder Netting Analysis**:
   - **Published Baseline**: 3.0 MW (HCM-2024-Q2, study date 04/2024).
   - **Prior Queue (Included in Baseline)**: Ravenford Data Services (PXA-2023-0894, 1.1 MW) and Fenwyn Industries Corp (PXA-2024-0091, 690 kW).
   - **Post-Publication Queue Additions**:
     - Grantwyck Solar LLC (PXA-2024-0231, 1.2 MW, queued 09/18/2024).
     - Cindermere Renewables LLC (PXA-2025-0044, 1.0 MW, queued 02/06/2025).
     - Total Post-Publication Queued Additions: 1.2 MW + 1.0 MW = 2.2 MW.
   - **Illustrative Remaining Headroom**: 3.0 MW − 2.2 MW = **0.8 MW (800 kW)**.
   - **Context**: Northline Renewables' requested capacity of 3.0 MW substantially exceeds this illustrative 0.8 MW headroom by 2.2 MW. This calculation is purely illustrative and non-binding.
4. **Mandatory Study Requirements**:
   - Pursuant to PLN-ALN-108-2024-10 Section 1, any project of consequential size—specifically a 3 MW pure-generation facility—strictly requires a **full feeder-level planning study** following standard Alleniom Distribution Planning methodology.
5. **Project-Specific Feasibility and System Reinforcements Pending**:
   - Determinations of project feasibility, reverse power flow limits at Elderpine Substation, thermal conductor ratings, zero-sequence overvoltage (3V0) protection requirements, and Direct Transfer Trip (DTT) telemetry remain **strictly PENDING** a current feeder-level assessment. This pending disposition is consistently maintained throughout the response.
6. **Disposition of Pre-Application Dialogue Meeting**:
   - The request for a pre-application dialogue meeting with Distribution Planning and the DER Program Office is **ACCEPTED**, with scheduling offered to take place immediately following completion of Distribution Planning's interim feeder review and priority refresh.
7. **Priority Hosting-Capacity Refresh Request**:
   - The DER Program Office has formally requested a **priority hosting-capacity refresh for Feeder ALN-108** from Distribution Planning, citing Regulatory Affairs Directive **RA-2025-07-01 Section 3.7**.
