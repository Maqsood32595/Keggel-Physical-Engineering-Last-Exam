# Engineering Review and Determination: Halbray Field Solar Site 3 Pre-PTO Backfeed Request

## 1. Executive Summary & Answer on Energization Date
In response to Imogen Strathe of Halbray Community Solar LP regarding the request to energize Halbray Field Solar Site 3 (PXA-2024-0002) from feeder ALN-159 on July 29, 2025 (07/29/2025) for pre-PTO backfeed:
- **Plain Answer**: Alleniom Utilities plainly states that **we cannot energize or cannot confirm energizing on 07/29/2025**.
- **Core Engineering Basis**: We cannot energize on 07/29/2025 on a breach of the adopted electrical code that the record shows in the installation itself, and on the express condition of Kelloway Township Electrical Permit EP-25-0418 prohibiting energization before final approval.

## 2. Specific Code Breaches Identified in the Physical Installation (NFPA 70-2023)
The electrical code adopted for the installation is **NFPA 70-2023** (adopted under Kelloway Township Ordinance 2024-03). Three specific code breaches are documented on the record:
1. **Type TC Tracker Drive Cable (NEC Article 336)**:
   - *Breach*: The electrical code adopted for the installation (NFPA 70-2023) requires Type TC cable to run in a raceway or cable tray, which the drive cable along the tracker row piles does not (shown as 3x12 AWG Cu Type TC open along row piles in Drawing E-203 Rev B).
   - *Required Action*: The tracker drive cable has to be put in a raceway or replaced with a wiring method the code permits along the rows, or shown so on the record, before 07/29/2025.
   - *Responsible Party*: Halbray Community Solar LP or its electrical contractor (Tamsworth Electrical Constructors Inc.) is the one to rework the tracker drive cable.
2. **Type USE-2 Inverter Output Conductors (NEC Article 338)**:
   - *Breach*: The electrical code adopted for the installation (NFPA 70-2023) requires Type USE cable above ground to come out of the ground into an outdoor enclosure, which the inverter output circuits on the skid frame never do (shown as 4/0 AWG and 1 AWG Type USE-2 in conduit on skid frame in Drawing E-301 Rev B).
   - *Required Action*: The inverter output conductors on the skids have to be replaced with a conductor type the code permits above ground, or shown so on the record, before 07/29/2025.
   - *Responsible Party*: Halbray Community Solar LP or its electrical contractor (Tamsworth Electrical Constructors Inc.) is the one to replace the inverter output conductors on the skids.
3. **Green-Enamel Strut Outdoors (NEC Section 300.6(A))**:
   - *Breach*: The electrical code adopted for the installation (NFPA 70-2023) requires steel supports outdoors to carry more corrosion protection than enamel alone, which the green-enamel strut at the array north edge does not (shown mounting AUX-1 and TC-1 to TC-8 in Drawing E-202 Rev B).
   - *Required Action*: The enamel strut has to be replaced or given corrosion protection suited to outdoors, or shown so on the record, before 07/29/2025.
   - *Responsible Party*: Halbray Community Solar LP or its electrical contractor (Tamsworth Electrical Constructors Inc.) is the one to replace the enamel strut.

## 3. Municipal Permitting Prerequisites (Permit EP-25-0418)
- Under Kelloway Township Electrical Permit EP-25-0418, the permitted work may not be energized before Kelloway Township's final approval of permit EP-25-0418.
- The final inspection booked for 07/24/2025 has to pass with Kelloway Township's written final approval in hand before 07/29/2025.
- Halbray Community Solar LP or its permit holder Tamsworth Electrical Constructors Inc. is the one to get the final inspection passed.
